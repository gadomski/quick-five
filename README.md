## Rules & Scoring

## Objective
Be the player with the highest score after the final round, which is triggered when someone reaches or exceeds **5,000 points**.

---

## Point Values

| Roll | Points |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Single `1`** | **100** points |
| **Single `5`** | **50** points |
| **Three-of-a-Kind** *(2s, 3s, 4s, 5s, 6s)* | **Number × 100** *(e.g., three 4s = 400 points)* |
| **Three `1`s** | **1,000** points |

Note: You must roll the Three-of-a-Kind in a single roll, they cannot be part of set aside dice

---

## How to Play

1. **Rolling:** Start your turn by rolling all **5 dice**.
2. **Keeping Points:** You **must** set aside at least **one scoring die** (or scoring combination) per roll to keep your points.
3. **Deciding to Continue or Stop:**
   - **Bank Your Points:** Stop your turn, add your points to your total, and pass all 5 dice to the next player.
   - **Push Your Luck:** Roll the remaining non-scoring dice to build your score higher. 
   - **Pass the Stack:** Instead of starting from zero, the next player can choose to inherit your banked points and roll *only the remaining dice* (unless it's the final round where dice must stay "alive").

> **Hot Dice!**  
> If **all 5 dice** become scoring dice during your turn, you get to pick up **all 5 dice** and keep rolling to add even more points to your turn!

---

## The Bust Rule
If you roll the remaining dice and **no scoring dice appear**, you "bust." You lose **all** unbanked points accumulated during that turn, and your turn ends immediately.

---

## Ending the Game
- **Triggering the End:** Once a player reaches or exceeds **5,000 points**, the final round begins.
- **The Last Round:** Every other player gets **one final turn** to try to beat the high score.
- **Winning:** During the final round, players cannot pass inherited dice—you must keep rolling until you choose to stop or bust. The player with the highest total score wins!
